English

This policy describes the Android version of SifirRia, provided by yusoffdev, with application ID com.yusoffdev.sifirria. It covers the current testing version, which uses Google test banner ads rather than production advertising.

No learner registration is required. Learning progress is stored locally by the app. Android advertising uses Google's SDK and involves network requests, so this is not a claim that the app processes no data.

1. Learning information stored by the app

SifirRia stores completed-mission achievements and best star ratings by multiplication table and difficulty, the selected language, and the sound preference in the app's private Android preferences. These are used to restore progress and preferences when the app is reopened. The animation preference and unfinished mission or mistake-review state are session-only.

The app has no learner accounts, developer-operated learning server, or in-app cloud-sync feature. Our learning code does not send individual answers or saved achievements to a developer backend or intentionally include them in ad requests.

The app does not request a learner's name, email, date of birth, school, payment details, or precise location. It does not request camera, microphone, contacts, SMS, or location permissions. Sound effects are playback only, not audio recording.

2. Android test advertising and Google

Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) may display a test banner on completed full-mission results. Ads do not gate lessons, hints or retries. The current implementation uses Google's sample banner ID, child-directed treatment, a G-rated ad-content setting, and non-personalized requests. The final Android manifest excludes the advertising-ID permission.

Google documents SDK processing of network/IP information, approximate location derived from IP, ad/app interactions, diagnostics, and applicable device identifiers for ad delivery, measurement and abuse prevention. Actual processing depends on SDK configuration. Test or non-personalized ads do not mean that no network data is processed. Google documents TLS protection for SDK data in transit.

See Google's Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads data disclosures. We do not control Google's retention or independently erase its records.

3. Children and family use

SifirRia is a multiplication-learning app intended for school-age learners, including children. No learner profile or direct messaging is provided. Parents or guardians should handle support inquiries and avoid sending a child's name, school, photos, or other unnecessary personal details. The app's ad configuration is described above; these settings are not a guarantee that all legal requirements in every country have been satisfied.

4. Support messages

If a parent, guardian or other user emails us, we receive the sender's email address and whatever information they choose to include. We use it to respond, investigate the reported issue and handle privacy requests, not to create a learner account. Messages are processed through our email provider. Do not send passwords, payment information or unnecessary information about children.

5. Sharing and security

We do not operate a service that sells learner progress or learner profiles. Information may be processed by Google through the advertising SDK, by our email provider when you contact us, or as necessary to comply with a valid legal obligation. Local progress uses Android's app-private storage; this is not a promise that the app independently encrypts it at rest. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed absolutely secure.

6. Retention, deletion and device backups

Local achievements and preferences remain until Android app storage is cleared, the app is uninstalled, or the operating system otherwise removes them. A parent or device owner can use Android Settings → Apps → SifirRia → Storage → Clear storage/data; labels vary by device. This resets local progress and preferences. Clearing cache alone is not the same as clearing app storage.

Device backups or device-to-device transfers may include app data depending on Android and the device owner's settings. SifirRia does not provide its own backup service; manage operating-system backup and restored copies through the relevant device/account controls.

Support messages are kept only as needed to resolve the inquiry, maintain necessary support records, or meet security and legal obligations. Contact us to request deletion of information sent directly to us. We cannot remotely erase app-private progress on your device; use the device controls above. Data independently processed by Google is governed by Google's policies and controls. There is no SifirRia learner account to delete.

7. Offline use, policy website and changes

Lessons and local learning progress work without an internet connection; loading ads requires a connection. This policy page contains no analytics scripts or advertising. When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security, as described in GitHub Pages documentation.

We will update this page and its date when our practices change. A move to production advertising or other new data processing requires the policy and relevant app disclosures to be reviewed first.

Bahasa Melayu

Dasar ini menerangkan versi Android SifirRia oleh yusoffdev, dengan ID aplikasi com.yusoffdev.sifirria. Versi ujian semasa menggunakan banner iklan ujian Google, bukan iklan produksi.

Tiada pendaftaran pelajar. Kemajuan pembelajaran disimpan secara lokal oleh aplikasi. Namun, SDK iklan Android membuat permintaan rangkaian; aplikasi ini bukan aplikasi yang langsung tidak memproses data.

1. Data pembelajaran

Aplikasi menyimpan pencapaian misi yang telah selesai, bintang terbaik mengikut sifir dan tahap, pilihan bahasa serta tetapan bunyi dalam storan persendirian aplikasi Android. Maklumat ini digunakan untuk memulihkan kemajuan dan tetapan apabila aplikasi dibuka semula. Tetapan animasi serta misi atau ulang kaji yang belum selesai hanya untuk sesi semasa.

Tiada akaun pelajar, pelayan pembelajaran yang dikendalikan pembangun atau fungsi penyegerakan awan dalam aplikasi. Kod pembelajaran tidak menghantar jawapan individu atau pencapaian tersimpan kepada pelayan pembangun, dan tidak sengaja memasukkannya dalam permintaan iklan.

Aplikasi tidak meminta nama, email, tarikh lahir, sekolah, maklumat pembayaran atau lokasi tepat pelajar. Tiada permintaan kebenaran kamera, mikrofon, kenalan, SMS atau lokasi. Bunyi hanya dimainkan, bukan dirakam.

2. Iklan ujian dan kanak-kanak

Banner ujian Google AdMob boleh dipaparkan pada keputusan misi penuh. Iklan tidak menjadi syarat untuk belajar, melihat bantuan atau mencuba semula. Permintaan dikonfigurasi untuk layanan kandungan kanak-kanak, penarafan G dan iklan tidak diperibadikan. Kebenaran advertising ID dikecualikan daripada manifest akhir.

SDK Google masih boleh memproses maklumat rangkaian/peranti, interaksi dan diagnostik seperti diterangkan dalam bahagian English dan pautan Google di atas. Iklan ujian bukan bermaksud tiada data rangkaian diproses. Ibu bapa atau penjaga hendaklah mengurus pertanyaan sokongan tanpa menghantar butiran peribadi kanak-kanak yang tidak diperlukan.

3. Sokongan, perkongsian dan keselamatan

Apabila anda menghantar email sokongan, kami menerima alamat email serta maklumat yang anda berikan untuk menjawab pertanyaan, menyiasat masalah dan mengurus permintaan privasi. Maklumat tidak digunakan untuk mencipta akaun pelajar. Email diproses melalui penyedia email kami. Kami tidak mengendalikan perkhidmatan yang menjual kemajuan atau profil pelajar. Data boleh diproses oleh penyedia yang diterangkan di atas atau sebagaimana diperlukan oleh kewajipan undang-undang yang sah.

Storan persendirian Android bukan jaminan bahawa aplikasi menyulitkan data secara berasingan. Tiada kaedah storan atau penghantaran yang boleh dijamin selamat sepenuhnya.

4. Penyimpanan dan pemadaman

Kemajuan lokal kekal sehingga storan aplikasi dikosongkan, aplikasi dinyahpasang atau data dibuang oleh sistem. Gunakan Tetapan Android → Aplikasi → SifirRia → Storan → Kosongkan storan/data. Nama menu mungkin berbeza. Tindakan ini menetapkan semula kemajuan dan tetapan. Mengosongkan cache sahaja tidak memadam semua data aplikasi.

Sandaran atau pemindahan data peranti mungkin memasukkan data aplikasi bergantung pada Android dan tetapan pemilik peranti. Urus salinan tersebut melalui kawalan sandaran sistem atau akaun yang berkaitan. Aplikasi tidak mempunyai perkhidmatan sandaran sendiri.

Email sokongan disimpan hanya selama diperlukan untuk menyelesaikan pertanyaan, rekod sokongan yang perlu atau kewajipan keselamatan/undang-undang. Hubungi kami untuk meminta pemadaman maklumat yang dihantar terus kepada kami. Kami tidak boleh memadam kemajuan lokal pada peranti dari jauh atau memadam rekod Google secara bebas. Tiada akaun pelajar SifirRia untuk dipadam.

5. Laman polisi dan perubahan

Pembelajaran dan kemajuan lokal boleh digunakan tanpa internet; iklan memerlukan sambungan. Halaman dasar ini tidak memasang skrip analitik atau iklan. Jika dihoskan di GitHub Pages, GitHub merekod alamat IP pelawat bagi tujuan keselamatan. Perubahan amalan akan disertai kemas kini dasar dan tarikh ini. Pengaktifan iklan produksi memerlukan semakan semula dasar serta pendedahan aplikasi.
